## Ecopoetry {#ecopoetry}

With the poet [Daniel J Townsend](https://www.moveslowmakethings.com/) I co-host the Ecopoetic Workshop Series: free creative writing workshops where scientific understanding meets poetry, open to researchers, poets and anyone who loves language.
